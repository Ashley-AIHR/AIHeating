`yinchuan-osm-source.json` is the original Overpass geographic extract. `yinchuan.json` is a deterministic adaptation made with `scripts/prepare-yinchuan.mjs`, containing local display coordinates, source feature IDs and tags. Its SHA-256 identifies the input extract and its timestamp identifies the source database snapshot.

Query extent: latitude 38.438–38.454, longitude 106.250–106.272 (WGS84). Query: building ways, highway ways and water ways with geometry. Endpoint: https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter

Assumed heights are labelled per feature; heights derived from levels use an assumed 3 metres per level. B01–B12 are synthetic demonstrator associations, not OSM attributes or verified heating-service identities. Heating connections, station symbol, visual facades and thermal parameters are illustrative, not surveyed utility assets. This extract does not establish the boundary of 保伏桥新村A区.
